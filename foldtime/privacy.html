FoldTime

Privacy Policy

Effective November 28, 2026

The short version

FoldTime doesn't collect any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything FoldTime records stays on your iPhone. The one exception is the optional leaderboard: if you choose to join it, your open counts go to Apple's Game Center, and nothing else does.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your fold log: the time of each open and fold, where it came from (the Shortcuts automation, the live hinge, or filled in automatically), and the battery level at that moment. It's stored in FoldTime's private storage on your device and shared only with FoldTime's own widgets.
  • Hinge readings: the live angle is read only while FoldTime is on screen. Opening Test results are saved on your device. The PDF you can create is made on your iPhone, and it goes only where you choose to share it.
  • Screen Time: if you allow it, FoldTime uses Apple's Screen Time framework to estimate which apps you use folded and open. That data is shown inside a protected Apple report view. FoldTime can't read it, save it or send it anywhere, and it is never included in share images, widgets or Siri answers.
  • Settings, badges, Hinge Safe times and your light colour are stored on your device.
  • Notifications (the weekly report and the end of your trial) are scheduled on your device. FoldTime doesn't use a push notification server.

Sharing

FoldTime shares nothing unless you tap a share button. Duo Wrapped cards and Hinge Safe times are made only from your fold log and game results, never from Screen Time data, and you decide where they go.

Leaderboard (optional)

FoldTime has an optional Game Center leaderboard. It's off unless you join, from Today or Settings › Leaderboard, and until then FoldTime doesn't sign in to Game Center or send anything.

  • What's sent: only three numbers, your opens today, this week and all time, under your Game Center nickname.
  • What's never sent: Screen Time data, app names, the times of your folds, or anything else from your fold log.
  • Who handles it: Apple's Game Center. Other players see your nickname and counts on the boards. FoldTime has no server of its own and never receives this data. How Apple handles Game Center data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Leaving: turn it off in Settings › Leaderboard. FoldTime stops sending right away. Counts already on the Today and This Week boards stay until those boards reset.

The leaderboard data isn't linked to your identity by FoldTime and isn't used for tracking.

Purchases

The 3-day Trial, FoldTime Pro and tips are handled by Apple through the App Store. FoldTime learns only whether a purchase is active on your device. It never sees your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Siri and Shortcuts

FoldTime's actions run on your iPhone. Answers use only your fold log. How Apple processes Siri requests is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

FoldTime doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting FoldTime removes its data from your iPhone. You can also delete individual hinge tests in the app.

Changes

If FoldTime ever sends anything else off your device, this policy will be updated first, and the feature will be off until you turn it on.
