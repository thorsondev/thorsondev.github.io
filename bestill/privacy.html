Be Still

Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

The short version

Be Still doesn't collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you write in the app stays on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account, which we can't see.

What stays on your device

  • Your notes, highlights, bookmarks, prayers, journal entries, reading plans and memory verses are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for Be Still, Apple syncs them between your own devices through your private iCloud account. We have no access to it.
  • Bible Chat answers your questions with a language model that runs on your iPhone: Apple Intelligence, or an optional model you download once. Your questions and its answers are never sent to us or to anyone else. Saved conversations are encrypted on your device.
  • Speaking a question to Bible Chat uses Apple's speech recognition on your device. The microphone listens only after you tap it, while you speak.
  • Today's Reflection is written on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence. Nothing about it leaves your device.
  • Your reading streak and the days you open the app are counted on your device only.
  • Reminders (verse of the day, streak and reading plans) are scheduled on your device. Be Still doesn't use a push notification server.

What Be Still downloads

To show you the Bible and its tools, the app requests content from these services. Each request says only what to fetch, such as a chapter, a translation or a voice. Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address when it connects. Be Still sends them nothing about you.

  • Free Use Bible API (bible.helloao.org): Bible text, such as the Berean Standard Bible, plus study notes and cross-references.
  • ESV API by Crossway: the English Standard Version, if you choose it.
  • API.Bible: other translations, if you choose them.
  • Hugging Face: the optional natural voices and the optional Bible Chat model, downloaded once if you ask for them.

Apple Health and Face ID

  • If you allow it, Be Still saves your quiet times to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. It never reads any of your health data.
  • Face ID, which can lock your Journal and Bible Chat, is handled entirely by iOS. Be Still never sees your face data.

Tips

Be Still is free. Optional tips are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account.

Children

Be Still is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Everything you've written lives on your devices. You can delete individual entries in the app, clear all Bible Chat conversations from its menu, or delete the app to remove everything from that device. To remove synced data, delete the app's data from iCloud in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
