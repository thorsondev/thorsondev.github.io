Common questions

Why don't I see Today's Reflection or Bible Chat?

Today's Reflection is written by Apple Intelligence, so it needs an iPhone or iPad that supports it, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. Bible Chat can also use its own model, downloaded once, on devices without Apple Intelligence.

How do I sync my notes and prayers between devices?

Sign in to iCloud on each device and make sure iCloud is allowed for Be Still in Settings. Your notes, highlights, prayers and plans sync through your own private iCloud account.

Can I read without an internet connection?

Yes. Download a translation in Settings › Offline Library, and it's there whenever you need it, along with full-text search.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Go to Settings › Reminders in the app. You can choose a time for the verse of the day, an evening streak reminder and a morning reading plan reminder, each on its own. You can also tap the bell on the streak card or on a reading plan.

Which translations are available?

The Berean Standard Bible and other free translations are always available, and the ESV can be chosen in Settings. The NIV is coming soon.

I forgot my Journal lock. How do I get in?

The lock uses Face ID or your device passcode, so anyone who can unlock your iPhone can unlock the Journal. Nothing is stored with Be Still that could be reset.

Does Be Still cost anything?

No. Everything is free, with no ads and nothing locked. Tips are optional and unlock nothing.
