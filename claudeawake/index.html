Questions

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel. On macOS 26 and later it uses Apple's Liquid Glass design. Works with Claude Desktop and Claude Code.

Why does it ask for my password?

macOS always sleeps when you close the lid. To change that, ClaudeAwake adds one rule that lets it run exactly two commands: turn lid sleep off, and turn it back on. It can't do anything else with that permission, and you can remove it from Settings.

Why does auto-continue need Accessibility?

To send your continue message, ClaudeAwake pastes it into Claude and presses Return, the same as you would. macOS requires Accessibility permission for that. If you'd rather not allow it, everything else still works and you'll get a notification instead.

Is it safe to keep a closed MacBook awake?

ClaudeAwake lets your Mac sleep if it gets too hot or the battery drops below the level you choose (10% by default). Give your Mac some airflow on long runs, and avoid packing it tightly in a bag for hours.

What data does it collect?

None. It reads Claude's local log files on your Mac only to spot “limit resets at…” messages. If you turn on phone alerts, short notices like “Claude finished” go to ntfy.sh under a private random topic. There's no analytics and no account.
